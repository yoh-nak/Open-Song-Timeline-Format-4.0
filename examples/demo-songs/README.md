# OSTF 4.0 Golden Demo Songs

These 12 `.ostf` files are the official long-term musical reference set for OSTF 4.0.

They are intentionally separated from `../feature-fixtures/`: demo songs prioritize realistic musical integration, while fixtures cover exact boundary values and special semantic cases.

| # | Song | Length | Focus |
|---:|---|---:|---|
| 01 | Neon Crosswalk | 32 bars | Pop, Bank/Program, CC, Channel Mode, drums |
| 02 | Rust and Rain | 32 bars | Alternative Rock, simultaneous multiChannel guitar |
| 03 | Last Platform | 24 bars / 3/4 | Ballad, full lyric alignment, pressure and bend |
| 04 | Blue Lantern | 32 bars AABA | Jazz swing, extended harmony, walking bass |
| 05 | Above the Vector | 32 bars | Trance, multiple logical buses, CC automation |
| 06 | Velvet Corners | 32 bars | Neo Soul, multiple melody tracks, counter melody |
| 07 | Glass Garden | 24 bars | MPE Lower Zone, per-note expression |
| 08 | Orbiting Light | 24 bars | MPE Upper Zone, poly pressure |
| 09 | Two Sides of Morning | 36 bars | Lead/high/low vocals, shared lyric document |
| 10 | Answer Back | 32 bars | Call/response, separate lyric documents |
| 11 | Shifting Ground | 24 bars | 7/8, 4/4, 5/4, tempo/key change, fixed trigger |
| 12 | Signal Bloom | 40 bars | Complete OSTF 4.0 integrated showcase |

See `../../docs/DEMO_SONG_GUIDE.ja.md` and `../../docs/DEMO_QUALITY_AUDIT.ja.md` for design and audit details.
